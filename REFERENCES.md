# The Strata — Visual References (owner-supplied, 2026-06-10)

The owner supplied three reference images with the directive:

> "really beautiful crystalline structures that grow and glow in Solana hues,
> beautifully rendered and growing in and through space."

The image files live with the owner; this document is the canonical written analysis.
Any centerpiece work MUST be judged against these, not against generic "crystal" priors.

## Reference 1 — Mineral specimen collage (the vocabulary)

A collage of real specimens: amethyst clusters, aura quartz, opals, vivianite/azurite
blade clusters, pink tourmaline, druzy hearts, garnet-on-matrix. What it teaches:

- **Everything is an AGGREGATE.** No specimen is a single clean gem. Radiating blade
  clusters, families of terminated prisms at organic angles, botryoidal rounded masses,
  druzy fields of thousands of sub-millimeter crystals. A lone polyhedron is not in
  this vocabulary at all.
- **Saturated jewel hues** — royal violet-purple, magenta-pink, teal-green. This is
  literally the Solana brand axis: purple `#9945FF` ↔ green `#14F195`, plus magenta
  between them. Not pastel, not white-blue: SATURATED.
- **Finish variety on one object**: glassy gem faces + opal fire (micro-iridescent
  color speckle) + aura iridescence (metallic rainbow sheen) + druzy micro-sparkle
  (glitter as a *texture*, thousands of real facets catching light).
- The opal cabochons read like **galaxies inside stone** — the universe seen within
  the object. Directly on-theme for a piece about seeing a network inside a record.

## Reference 2 — Cobalto-calcite geode (the contrast)

A rough, dull grey-beige matrix shell cracked open to a glittering saturated-magenta
druzy cavity. What it teaches:

- **The beauty is the REVEAL**: plain mineral crust outside, jewel interior inside.
- **Sparkle is structural**, not a post effect — countless tiny crystal faces each
  catching the light separately.
- Maps perfectly to finality: **old, settled history = the quiet rough matrix; the
  living growth edge = the glowing druzy interior.**

## Reference 3 — Backlit amethyst geode (the glow + the render)

An upright geode standing in soft-focus ground, lit so it glows from within:
concentric zones from rock rim → blue-violet band → a **burning amber-orange band lit
like embers** → deep purple terminated crystal points in the cavity. What it teaches:

- **Concentric color ZONING** — bands of distinct hue, not a single gradient wash.
  (And its purple + amber is exactly the piece's existing palette.)
- **Internal light**: the glow comes from inside the object outward, not from rim
  lighting. "Grow and GLOW."
- **Photographic rendering**: shallow depth of field, soft environment, the object in
  focus — a photographed artifact, not a rendered sprite. (Model for a BokehPass.)

## Synthesis — what the centerpiece must become

A **growing crystalline cluster suspended in space**: many crystals, not one; nucleating
new glowing points/blades with the network's 400ms heartbeat; young growth as saturated
druzy/gem material in Solana purple–green with amber inner light; aging material deepening
and settling toward quiet dark matrix (finality as the geode shell); thousands of
micro-facets sparkling; rendered with real transmission/refraction, real environment
light, and photographic depth of field. It grows **in and through space** — directional,
alive, unbounded by a pedestal.

Candidate honest mappings (design latitude on which to use):
- new slot → a new crystal point nucleates at the growth front (the strike = nucleation flash)
- leader bar (4 slots) → one blade/cluster spur per leader, four growth pulses each
- missed slot → a dark vacancy in the lattice, never celebrated
- finality (~30 slots / ~12 s) → points fade from glowing gem to deep-set matrix
- the receding history → the matrix mass the cluster grows from, trailing through space
