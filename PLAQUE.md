## Wall placard (~190 words)

# THE STRATA
### The Solana blockchain, alive

Every fraction of a second, somewhere across the world, thousands of computers agree on
what just happened — and a new layer is added to a record that can never be rewritten.
You are watching that happen, live, right now.

This is the Solana network, drawn as a growing crystal. Each point of light is a real
validator: a computer, somewhere on Earth, helping keep the network honest. The drifting
color is real activity — money moving, trades, art, stake — streaming through the network
this second. With every heartbeat of agreement the crystal grows, and once a layer
settles it can never change again. It becomes part of the record, forever.

Nothing here is invented. It is all happening, now, on one of the most intricate machines
humanity has built — a machine that usually looks, to almost everyone, like a price chart.

It doesn't have to. Complex things can be made visible. And when the thing is this
beautiful underneath, it can be made beautiful to look at.
